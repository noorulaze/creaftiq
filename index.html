Six Visual Principles.

The non-negotiable operational standards governing every line of code, typography decision, and animation curve crafted at CRAFTIQ.

01 / REDUCTION

Radical Reduction

Every element must defend its right to occupy the viewport. We strip away decorative clutter, unnecessary shadows, and artificial gradients so content and structural form dominate.

PURITY OF INTENT NO NOISE
02 / SCALE

Monumental Type

Typography is architecture in two dimensions. We treat scale, tracking, and leading as spatial structures that command cognitive rhythm and enforce undeniable visual hierarchy.

HEROIC PROPORTION EDITORIAL WEIGHT
03 / MOTION

Kinetic Intent

Motion is choreography, never decoration. Every transition preserves spatial continuity, guides user attention, and applies physical weight and momentum to digital interactions.
